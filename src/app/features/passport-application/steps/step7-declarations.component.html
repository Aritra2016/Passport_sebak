Step 7 of 9

Statutory Self-Declaration

Mandatory declarations under the Passports Act, 1967. Suppressing information is an offence punishable by imprisonment.

1.
Have you ever been charged with any criminal proceedings pending before a court in India?
2.
Has any warrant or summons for your appearance been issued & pending before a court in India?
3.
Have you ever been convicted by a court in India for an offence with imprisonment of not less than two years?
4.
Have you ever been refused or denied a passport or travel document by the Government of India?
5.
Has your passport ever been impounded or revoked by any passport authority?
6.
Have you ever applied for or acquired citizenship of any other country, or returned to India on an Emergency Certificate?
You must read and agree to the statutory declaration to proceed.
